## How to read the competency pages

Each published competency follows the same general structure:

1. **What I am able to do** — the practical capability demonstrated.
2. **Professional knowledge** — the concepts relevant to the competency.
3. **Evidence** — the work supporting the capability.
4. **Quality and verification** — how the work was checked.
5. **Evidence boundaries** — what the evidence does and does not demonstrate.
6. **Professional capability demonstrated** — a concise evidence-based statement.

The detailed technical material is kept separately in **Technical Evidence** so that the main competency pages remain focused on professional capability rather than becoming a record of individual commands or procedures.
